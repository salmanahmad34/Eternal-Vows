Your invitation link remains active until the wedding date. Once the event completes, the invitation automatically archives.

Can I edit the date and location after paying?

Yes. You get 1 free design revision post-purchase to update details like timing or maps.

How do I share the invite link on WhatsApp?

Your dashboard will generate a dedicated shareable link. When copied and pasted into WhatsApp, it displays a premium title card and a short preview message automatically.
