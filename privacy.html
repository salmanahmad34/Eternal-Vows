Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Eternal Vowz, created by Salman Ahmad, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when registering, creating an invitation, or contacting us. This includes:

  • Names of the couple, wedding dates, and venue locations.
  • Email addresses used for account registration.
  • Any messages or quotes provided for the invitation templates.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the digital invitation service. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Generate your customized wedding invitation link.
  • Track page views (CTR) and RSVP responses for your dashboard analytics.
  • Process payments via Razorpay (we do not store credit card details).

3. Data Security & Storage

We use Supabase as our secure backend provider. Your data is stored securely and we implement standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access. The invitation link is public by design, meaning anyone with the link can view your wedding details.
